## STRUCTURE GUIDE

### 1. How We Met
- Where, when, and how you first met
- What your first impressions were
- When friendship became something more

*Example areas to cover:*
> "I first met [PARTNER] on [DATE] at [LOCATION]. We were introduced by [HOW]. I remember..."

---

### 2. Development of the Relationship
- How the relationship progressed
- Key milestones (first date, becoming exclusive, meeting families, moving in together)
- Any challenges you overcame together

---

### 3. Our Daily Life Together
- What a typical day/week looks like
- How you share household responsibilities
- How you support each other

---

### 4. Our Social Life as a Couple
- Friends and family who know you as a couple
- Events you attend together
- How you are recognised as a couple in your community

---

### 5. Our Financial Life
- Shared accounts, bills, expenses
- How you manage money as a couple

---

### 6. Our Commitment and Future Plans
- Why you want to build your life in Australia
- Your long-term plans together
- Why this relationship is genuine and enduring

---

## TIPS FROM CASE OFFICERS

- Be **specific** — dates, places, names, events
- Be **consistent** — your statement and your partner's must align on facts
- Be **genuine** — write naturally, not like a legal document
- Avoid **vague statements** like "we love each other" without examples
- Include **difficult times** you got through together — shows real relationship
- Length: Aim for **2–4 pages** per person
