# Statutory Declaration Guide (Form 888)
## AU Partner Visa — Supporting Persons

> Form 888 must be completed by people who know you and your partner as a couple.
> They must be Australian citizens or permanent residents.
> Minimum 2 required — aim for 3–4 from different parts of your life.

---

## WHO TO ASK

Good candidates:
- Close mutual friends
- Family members (your family OR partner's family)
- Colleagues who know you both
- Neighbours who see you together

Avoid:
- People who have never met you as a couple
- People who live overseas (must be AU citizen/PR)

---

## WHAT THEY NEED TO INCLUDE

The declarant should address as many of these as possible:

1. **Who they are** — name, occupation, how long they've known you
2. **How they know you as a couple** — how/when they first met you together
3. **How long they've known you as a couple**
4. **Evidence of your relationship** — what they've personally witnessed:
   - Seeing you live together
   - Social events you attended as a couple
   - How you interact / treat each other
   - Your plans for the future
5. **Their opinion** on the genuineness of your relationship

---

## SAMPLE TEXT FOR DECLARANT

> The declarant writes this themselves, but you can share this guide with them.

---

*"I, [DECLARANT FULL NAME], make this statutory declaration in support of the partner visa application of Pratik [LAST NAME] and [PARTNER NAME].*

*I am an Australian [citizen/permanent resident], born on [DATE], and work as [OCCUPATION].*

*I have known Pratik and [PARTNER] as a couple since [DATE/YEAR]. I met them through [HOW — e.g., mutual friends, work, neighbourhood]. Over the past [X] years I have socialised with them regularly, including [specific examples: birthday parties, dinners, holidays, family gatherings].*

*I have observed that they share a home at [ADDRESS — optional] and conduct themselves in every way as a committed couple. They [specific observations: cook together, support each other, make decisions jointly, etc.].*

*I am fully satisfied that the relationship between Pratik and [PARTNER] is genuine, loving, and long-term. I believe they are committed to a shared future in Australia.*

*I make this solemn declaration conscientiously believing the contents to be true, under the [relevant state/territory] Oaths Act."*

---

## SIGNING REQUIREMENTS

The Form 888 must be:
- Signed in front of an **authorised witness** (JP, police officer, lawyer, etc.)
- Witnessed signature dated
- Declarant must bring photo ID
